Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-09

This Privacy Policy describes how the Emma mobile app ("Emma", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information. It applies to the Emma Android app and the voice services it connects to.

Summary: We access the microphone only when you use voice features. Voice and text inputs may be sent to Emma servers and AI providers (such as OpenAI) to generate responses. Your vault data and settings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information We Collect

  • Microphone audio when you activate voice features.
  • Text you type or speak during conversations.
  • Memory content and attachments you save to your vault.
  • App settings and preferences stored on your device.
  • Basic network and diagnostic metadata needed to run the service.

How We Use Information

  • Provide voice and chat responses.
  • Save and organize your memories locally on your device.
  • Maintain reliability, security, and performance of the service.

Microphone and Voice Features

Emma requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission so you can speak to the app. Audio is captured only when you press the microphone control or start a voice session. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in Android settings.

Sharing and Third Parties

To provide AI and voice features, we send voice or text inputs to service providers such as OpenAI. We may also use hosting providers to operate our backend services. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Storage and Retention

Your vault data is stored locally on your device until you delete it. Data sent to servers for voice and AI processing is used to provide the service and may be retained for a limited period for reliability and security.

Your Choices

  • Disable microphone access in system settings.
  • Delete or export your local vault data at any time.
  • Stop using voice features without losing access to the rest of the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/emma-hml/emma-lite .
